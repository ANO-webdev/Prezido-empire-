Our Story

Abuja's Sweetest
Little Empire

Tucked in the warm, welcoming neighbourhood of Pyakasa in Lugbe, Prezido Cakes Empire was born from a simple dream — to fill every table, every celebration, and every heart with cakes as beautiful as they are delicious.

We believe a great cake is more than flour and sugar. It's the colours of a child's first birthday, the elegance of a couple's first slice, the warmth of a family gathered together. Every order we take is an honour we do not take lightly.

  • Fresh ingredients baked to order — never stale, never rushed
  • Custom designs crafted to match your vision exactly
  • Friendly service from order to delivery
  • Serving Lugbe, Abuja and surrounding areas
